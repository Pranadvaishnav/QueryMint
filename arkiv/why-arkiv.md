# Why Arkiv

QueryMint needs publisher-owned records that independent applications can query without receiving a private database credential. Arkiv gives each record a wallet owner, indexed query attributes, public read access, and an explicit expiry. That lets a buyer inspect publisher attribution and lets publishers retain control of updates and deletion while making discovery composable across apps.

Arkiv does not enforce QueryMint's commercial license or keep data private. Whitechain enforces payment authorization; sensitive payloads are encrypted before storage and released after confirmed settlement. Query completeness and delivery remain coordinator trust assumptions.
